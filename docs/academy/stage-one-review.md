# Stage 1 review — connect the foundations

This is a teaching/retrieval guide, not the final Stage Assessment or a permission to begin Stage 2. Use fresh examples and ask for a reason, a condition and a limitation. Instructor feedback determines the next practice; reading this page grants no completion.

1. **Money:** Describe paying, comparing and retaining funds in a new everyday story. Explain what changes when the nominal amount stays fixed but prices change.
2. **History:** Compare two documented monetary arrangements in their own time/place. What problem did each address, what limitation remained, and why should we avoid turning selected examples into one universal historical ladder?
3. **The money system:** Trace a hypothetical account payment, distinguishing customer balances, payment instructions and bank settlement. Contrast commercial and central-bank roles with jurisdiction qualifications. Explain a simplified loan/deposit creation example and its constraints without claiming limitless lending.
4. **Prices and rates:** State a basket, base and period before calculating a change. Distinguish the inflation rate from the price level and nominal amounts from buying power. Explain the assumptions in simple interest, and why a causal/policy story needs more evidence than one price observation.
5. **Markets:** Connect an issuer, saver/investor, borrower/lender and risk manager to different needs. Distinguish new financing from secondary resale. What does liquidity help with, and what does it not guarantee?
6. **Decisions:** Compare investing, trading and gambling-like behavior by process, assumptions and risk, not only horizon or outcome. Give a defensible no-action decision and explain its opportunity cost.
7. **Price formation:** Show both sides of an executed trade. Change willingness, order depth or expectations in a hypothetical example; distinguish a plausible conditional explanation from a confident forecast. What information is missing?
8. **FX:** Map a new cross-border currency need and distinguish the parties’ purposes, relative value and delivery risks. Explain what remains to be learned about quotes and execution in Stage 2 without pretending that conversion guarantees profit.

Use the permanent class guides for vocabulary and worked review examples. Use class-specific reinforcement for a precise weakness; do not automatically repeat all eight classes. Keep knowledge, reasoning, application and instructor-reviewed homework distinct. Chart demonstrations, final assessment and stage graduation are not inferred from this review.

## Instructor quality check

Ask the learner to explain a changed condition, not repeat a memorized example. Record feedback in the existing review workflow. If additional practice is required, point to the mapped concept and a fresh case. Keep the reserved final-assessment scenario and private rubrics out of this review.
